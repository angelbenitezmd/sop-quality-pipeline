I. Purpose

The purpose of this standard operating procedure is the definition of the methodology for
the performance of the quantitative determination of bacterial endotoxin in sterile
injectable drug products through the application of the kinetic chromogenic Limulus
Amebocyte Lysate (LAL) technique, such that the demonstration of conformance to the
established endotoxin limit is provided in support of the disposition of the associated
batches and in support of the protection of the patient from the occurrence of a pyrogenic
response.

II. Scope

The applicability of this procedure extends to the performance of endotoxin testing of all
finished sterile drug products, of Water-for-Injection, and of in-process solutions for
which the establishment of an endotoxin limit has been provided, and the conduct of the
test must be performed through the use of the kinetic chromogenic methodology as the
primary methodology, whereby, in the event that interference is encountered that cannot be
overcome through the performance of dilution up to the maximum valid dilution, an assessment
of the suitability of an alternative photometric technique must be conducted and the
selection of that alternative must be justified and documented in a manner that is
consistent with the risk-management principles that are described in ICH Q9 prior to the
adoption of the alternative for routine testing.

III. Responsibilities

The QC analyst must undertake the performance of the assay, the preparation of the
endotoxin standard and of the sample dilutions, and the documentation of all observations in
accordance with SOP-DOC-001. The QC reviewer must conduct an assessment of the completeness
and of the correctness of the data package prior to the approval of the reported result. The
QC Laboratory Manager must ensure the provision of adequate training, the maintenance of the
qualified status of the microplate reader and of the incubating reader software, and, in a
timely manner, must perform a review of any deviation that arises during the execution of
this procedure.

IV. Materials, Reagents, and Instrumentation

The following must be available for the conduct of the assay: a temperature-controlled
incubating microplate reader that is capable of the maintenance of 37 ± 1 °C and of the
measurement of absorbance at the wavelength that is specified by the reagent manufacturer;
kinetic chromogenic LAL reagent; Control Standard Endotoxin (CSE) the potency of which has
been established against the Reference Standard Endotoxin; LAL Reagent Water that is free of
detectable endotoxin; depyrogenated glassware or certified pyrogen-free labware; and a
validated data-reduction package for the calculation of the endotoxin concentration through
the construction of the standard curve. The verification of the calibration of the
analytical balance that is used for any gravimetric preparation must be confirmed by
reference to SOP-QC-006 before any weighing is undertaken.

V. Performance of Assay Validation and Verification of Suitability

Prior to the reporting of any result for a given product, the performance of a verification
of the absence of interfering factors must be undertaken through the conduct of a test for
inhibition and enhancement, whereby the recovery of a known quantity of endotoxin that is
added to the sample must be demonstrated to lie within the range of 50 percent to 200
percent, and, in the event that the recovery is found to lie outside of this range, the
performance of further dilution of the sample up to the maximum valid dilution must be
undertaken so that the demonstration of the overcoming of the interference is provided
before the establishment of the product as suitable for routine testing is confirmed. The
maximum valid dilution must be calculated through the relationship of the endotoxin limit,
the concentration of the product, and the labelled sensitivity of the reagent, and the
determination of the endotoxin limit must be performed through the application of the
threshold pyrogenic dose in relation to the maximum human dose per kilogram per hour.

VI. Procedure

1. The reconstitution of the Control Standard Endotoxin must be performed in accordance with
   the certificate of analysis, and the performance of vigorous mixing for the period that is
   specified must be undertaken so that the achievement of a homogeneous suspension is
   provided.
2. The preparation of the standard curve must be performed through the execution of a series
   of serial dilutions of the Control Standard Endotoxin that spans the range that is
   specified by the reagent manufacturer.
3. The preparation of the sample dilutions and of the positive product controls must be
   performed such that the positive product control receives a known spike of endotoxin at a
   concentration near the midpoint of the standard curve.
4. The dispensing of the samples, of the standards, and of the controls into the microplate,
   and the subsequent addition of the LAL reagent, must be performed within the incubating
   reader, and the initiation of the kinetic measurement must proceed without delay.
5. Upon the completion of the run, the review of the standard curve must be performed, and
   the acceptability of the run must be established through the demonstration that the
   correlation coefficient of the standard curve is not less than the value that is specified
   in USP <85>, that the recovery of the positive product control lies within the acceptance
   range, and that the negative control exhibits no detectable endotoxin.

VII. Calculation and Acceptance Criteria

The calculation of the endotoxin concentration must be performed through the interpolation
of the onset time of each sample against the standard curve, with the incorporation of the
applicable dilution factor. The interpretation of the result must be performed in accordance
with USP <85>, and the reporting of an endotoxin concentration that does not exceed the
established endotoxin limit for the product is considered to represent a conforming outcome.
Any occurrence of a result that exceeds the endotoxin limit must be subjected to an
investigation in a timely manner.

VIII. Data Review and Documentation

The documentation of all raw data, of the standard curve, of the control recoveries, and of
the calculation of the maximum valid dilution must be maintained within the validated data
system, and the performance of a second-person review must be undertaken prior to the
approval of the result in accordance with SOP-DOC-001.
