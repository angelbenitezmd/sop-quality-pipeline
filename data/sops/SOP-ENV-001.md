## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines the environmental monitoring (EM) program for the aseptic
processing areas of Building 4. It exists so that microbial and particulate control
is verified during routine production and at rest.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all Grade A, Grade B, Grade C, and Grade D areas that support
sterile fill-finish. It covers viable air, non-viable particle, surface, and personnel
monitoring.

## 3. Responsibilities

EM analysts must collect samples per the sampling plan. Production supervisors must
grant timely access to the areas. The EM Program Lead must review trends monthly.
Quality Assurance must approve every alert and action limit.

## 4. Sample Sites and Frequency

1. Follow the approved room-specific sampling map for each grade.
2. Monitor Grade A and Grade B zones on every production shift.
3. Monitor Grade C areas at least once per shift during processing.
4. Monitor Grade D areas at least weekly.
5. Record each site, cycle, and time stamp on the EM worksheet.

## 5. Sampling Methods

1. Collect viable air samples with a calibrated active air sampler.
2. Expose settle plates for no more than four hours in Grade A and Grade B.
3. Take surface samples with contact plates or swabs after processing.
4. Perform personnel monitoring on gloves and gowns at the end of each aseptic session.
5. Measure non-viable particles at 0.5 µm and 5.0 µm using a calibrated particle counter.

## 6. Alert and Action Limits

1. Compare each result against the alert and action limits in the EM limits table.
2. Notify the shift supervisor when a result meets or exceeds an alert limit.
3. Escalate every action-limit excursion to Quality Assurance within one hour.
4. Confirm that limits meet the classifications in EU GMP Annex 1 (2022 revision).

## 7. Data Review and Response

1. Read plates only after full incubation at both incubation temperatures.
2. Record colony counts and identify recovered isolates to the required level.
3. Correlate excursions with cleaning records generated under SOP-CLN-001.
4. Support product disposition with sterility results from SOP-QC-002.
5. Review and file all EM records per SOP-DOC-001.
