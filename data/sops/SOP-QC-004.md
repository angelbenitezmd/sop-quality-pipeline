I. Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is the definition of the methodology for the performance of
the determination of Total Organic Carbon (TOC) and of conductivity in Water-for-Injection
(WFI), such that the demonstration of conformance to the compendial quality attributes of
the water is provided in support of the release of the water for use in manufacturing and
in analytical operations.

II. Scope

The applicability of this procedure extends to the performance of TOC and conductivity
testing of WFI that is drawn from the sampling points of the WFI generation and
distribution system, the operation of which is described in SOP-EQP-004, and it governs the
conduct of both the routine monitoring of the system and the qualification-related testing
of the water.

III. Responsibilities

The QC analyst must undertake the performance of the collection of the sample, the
operation of the TOC analyser and of the conductivity meter, and the documentation of all
observations in accordance with SOP-DOC-001. The QC reviewer must conduct an assessment of
the completeness of the record prior to the approval of the result. The QC Laboratory
Manager must ensure the maintenance of the qualified status of the instrumentation.

IV. Materials and Instrumentation

The following must be available for the conduct of the testing: a TOC analyser that is
suitable for the measurement of low-level organic carbon; a conductivity meter equipped with
a temperature-compensated or non-temperature-compensated cell as appropriate; TOC system-
suitability standards consisting of a sucrose solution and a 1,4-benzoquinone solution;
low-TOC reagent water for the preparation of blanks; and pre-cleaned, low-organic sample
vessels. The performance of a verification of the system suitability of the TOC analyser
must be undertaken prior to the analysis of any sample.

V. Procedure

1. The collection of the sample must be performed through the flushing of the sampling point
   for the prescribed period and the subsequent filling of a low-organic sample vessel in a
   manner that provides the minimisation of the introduction of extraneous carbon.
2. The performance of the conductivity determination must be undertaken through the
   application of the compendial three-stage approach, whereby the measurement of the
   conductivity of the non-temperature-compensated sample is compared against the stage-one
   limit that corresponds to the measured temperature.
3. The performance of the TOC determination must be undertaken through the introduction of
   the sample into the TOC analyser and the acquisition of the organic-carbon response.
4. The performance of a system-suitability determination of the TOC analyser must be
   undertaken through the analysis of the sucrose standard and of the 1,4-benzoquinone
   standard, and the calculation of the response efficiency must be performed.

VI. Acceptance Criteria

The conformance of the water is established through the demonstration that the TOC does not
exceed 500 ppb (0.50 mg/L) and that the conductivity does not exceed the stage-one limit
that corresponds to the temperature of the measurement. The verification of the suitability
of the TOC analyser is established through the demonstration that the response efficiency
lies within the range of 85 percent to 115 percent. The qualification of the analytical
instrumentation must be maintained in accordance with USP <1058>.

VII. Data Review and Documentation

The documentation of all measurements, of the sampling-point identification, and of the
instrument-suitability outcomes must be maintained, and the performance of a second-person
review must be undertaken prior to the approval of the result in accordance with
SOP-DOC-001.
