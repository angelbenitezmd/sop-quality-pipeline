## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how the operator will perform the aseptic filling of sterile
injectable product into glass vials within the Grade A filling zone of Building 4. It exists
so that product sterility will be maintained throughout setup, filling, and stoppering, and
so that each filled batch will meet its defined quality attributes.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all aseptic filling of injectable vials on the Grade A/B filling
lines. The operator will apply this procedure to routine commercial batches and to
engineering batches as required. Aseptic process simulation is covered separately and will be
performed periodically to confirm continued control.

## 3. Responsibilities

The filling operator will execute the steps described here and will document each activity in
a timely manner. The line lead will verify that line clearance is completed before filling
begins. The shift supervisor will confirm that only qualified, currently gowned personnel are
present in the aseptic core. Records will be reviewed by the area supervisor in accordance
with SOP-DOC-001.

## 4. Materials and Equipment

The operator will use the qualified vial filling machine operated in accordance with
SOP-EQP-001, sterilized filling needles and product-contact tubing, depyrogenated glass
vials, sterilized stoppers, sterile-filtered process nitrogen, and the sterilizing-grade
product filter. Only components released by Quality Control will be used. Component contact
times and hold times will be observed as required.

## 5. Safety Warnings

The operator will observe the following safety warnings at all times:

1. **Sharps hazard.** Filling needles and stopper-bowl tooling are sharp. The operator will
   handle needles with the guard engaged and will discard used needles into the designated
   sharps container immediately after use.
2. **Compressed gas / asphyxiation.** Nitrogen is used for headspace overlay and can displace
   oxygen. The operator will confirm that room oxygen monitors are active before entry and
   will evacuate immediately if a low-oxygen alarm sounds.
3. **Flammable agents.** 70% IPA used for surface disinfection is flammable. The operator will
   keep IPA away from ignition sources and will allow surfaces to dry before energizing
   equipment.
4. **Moving parts / pinch points.** The star wheel, conveyor, and stoppering head present pinch
   and entanglement hazards. The operator will apply lockout/tagout before clearing a jam and
   will never place hands into a running machine.
5. **Aseptic intervention risk.** Any intervention into the Grade A zone increases
   contamination risk to the operator's own gowned barrier and to the product. The operator
   will minimize interventions, will use validated slow, deliberate movements, and will notify
   the line lead before any inherent or corrective intervention.
6. **Ergonomics and glass.** Broken vials create a glass hazard. The operator will use the
   designated tools to remove glass and will not reach across the filling point.

## 6. Procedure

1. Line clearance will be verified and the batch record will be reviewed for completeness
   before any component is introduced.
2. The operator will disinfect gloved hands and Grade A surfaces with 70% IPA using a suitable
   overlapping technique and will allow adequate contact time.
3. The sterilizing-grade product filter will be installed and a pre-use filter integrity test
   will be performed; the result will be recorded.
4. The operator will load depyrogenated vials onto the infeed and will confirm that the
   environment is being monitored in accordance with SOP-ENV-001.
5. Fill weight will be set to the target and the operator will perform initial fill-weight
   checks until the process is confirmed to be within limits.
6. Filling will be commenced and vials are stoppered as they exit the filling point; the
   operator will observe the line periodically for missing or misaligned stoppers.
7. Environmental and personnel monitoring samples are collected during the run as necessary
   and will be submitted to the laboratory.
8. At the end of filling, a post-use filter integrity test will be performed and the result
   recorded. Reconciliation of vials will be completed in a timely manner.

## 7. In-Process Controls and Acceptance Criteria

Fill weight will be checked at defined intervals and results will remain within the approved
range. Container closure will be assessed to confirm that stoppers are fully seated. Product
sterility will be confirmed by testing performed under SOP-QC-002. Any excursion will be
documented and evaluated as required before the batch proceeds.
