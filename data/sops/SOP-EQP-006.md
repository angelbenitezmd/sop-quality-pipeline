## Purpose

This procedure describes the preventive maintenance (PM) of the filling line isolators (Assets EQP-ISO-01 and EQP-ISO-02). It exists so that glove and sleeve integrity, pressure control, and the bio-decontamination cycle remain reliable and continue to protect the Grade A filling zone.

## Scope

This procedure applies to the scheduled PM of the isolators that enclose the vial filling line. It applies to engineering technicians who perform PM and to the operators who perform the pre-use glove checks.

## Responsibilities

Engineering technicians should perform the scheduled PM tasks. Operators should perform the daily glove inspection before production. The Senior Process Engineer should verify PM completion before the isolator is released.

## Equipment and Materials

- Isolator glove/sleeve integrity tester
- Replacement gloves and sleeves of the qualified type
- Vaporized hydrogen peroxide (VHP) consumables for the decontamination cycle
- Calibrated pressure and airflow references

## Procedure

Step 1: Confirm that the isolator is empty, powered down for maintenance, and released to Engineering, and record the asset number in the maintenance log.

Step 2: Inspect each glove and sleeve for pinholes, thinning, and seating, then perform the automated pressure-decay integrity test. Any glove that fails should be replaced with a qualified glove and retested before use.

Step 3: Verify the isolator pressure decay and airflow against the qualified setpoints, and confirm that the differential-pressure alarms function correctly.

Step 4: Perform a VHP bio-decontamination cycle and confirm that the cycle completes within its qualified parameters and that the aeration endpoint returns the residual peroxide to the acceptable limit.

Step 5: Record all results in the PM record, apply the PM-complete status label, and release the isolator. The Senior Process Engineer should review and sign the record before production resumes.

## Acceptance Criteria

The isolator should be released only when all gloves and sleeves pass the integrity test, the pressure and airflow are within the qualified ranges, and the VHP cycle and aeration endpoint are confirmed to meet their limits.
