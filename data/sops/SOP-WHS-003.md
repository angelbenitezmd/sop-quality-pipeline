## Purpose

This procedure covers segregation, holding, and disposal of waste generated at Building 4. It applies to general, hazardous, and rejected product waste.

## Scope

Applies to all waste handled by the warehouse and by disposal contractors.

## Responsibility

Warehouse staff should segregate waste at the point of generation. The Warehouse Manager should ensure that disposal contractors are approved. Quality Assurance should approve destruction of rejected product.

## Waste Categories

- General waste
- Hazardous and chemical waste
- Rejected or returned drug product
- Confidential printed material, such as labels and cartons

## Segregation

Waste should be segregated by category at the point of generation. Containers should be labeled clearly. Incompatible waste should not be mixed.

## Storage

Waste should be held in a designated area. The area should be secured and kept in a suitable condition. Waste should not accumulate beyond a reasonable level.

## Disposal

Waste should be collected by an approved contractor at a suitable frequency. Hazardous waste should be manifested. Rejected drug product should be destroyed in a manner that prevents recovery. Destruction should be witnessed and documented.

## Records

Disposal records and destruction certificates should be retained as required.
