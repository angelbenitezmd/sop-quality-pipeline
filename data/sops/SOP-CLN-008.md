PURPOSE AND APPLICABILITY

The purpose of this standard operating procedure is to provide a description of the disinfectant
efficacy qualification program that shall be applied at Building 4 for the demonstration of the
adequacy of the disinfectants and the sporicidal agent that are utilized in the classified
cleanrooms, and it shall be understood that the performance of the qualification of the
disinfectants, which includes the conduct of the evaluation of the reduction of the populations
of the challenge microorganisms that are recovered from the representative surface coupons
following the application of the disinfectant for the contact time that is under evaluation,
shall be undertaken only by personnel who have received the provision of the appropriate
training and who have been subject to the determination of competence in the execution of the
associated microbiological techniques.

SCOPE

This procedure shall be applied to the qualification of all disinfectants and the sporicidal
agent that are subject to the requirements for rotation and preparation that are set forth in
SOP-CLN-006, and it shall be applied to the qualification of the efficacy against the
representative panel of challenge microorganisms on the representative surface materials that
are considered to be appropriate for the classified areas, and it shall be applied as necessary
upon the introduction of a new disinfectant, upon the occurrence of a change to the
formulation, and at a periodic frequency that is deemed to be adequate.

RESPONSIBILITIES

It is the responsibility of the validation personnel that the execution of the qualification
studies and the provision of documentation of the results shall be undertaken in accordance
with this procedure, and it is the responsibility of the Quality Control function that the
performance of the enumeration of the challenge microorganisms shall be carried out in
accordance with SOP-QC-001, and it is the responsibility of the Validation Lead that the
review of the acceptability of the results and the approval of the qualification report shall
be provided in a timely manner.

MATERIALS AND EQUIPMENT

The following shall be utilized: the disinfectant or sporicidal agent that is subject to the
undertaking of the qualification, representative surface coupons that are constructed of the
materials that are present within the classified areas, suspensions of the challenge
microorganisms that are prepared to a concentration that is considered to be appropriate,
neutralizing agents that are subject to the confirmation of the effectiveness of the
neutralization, and the associated microbiological media and incubation equipment.

QUALIFICATION PROCEDURE

1. The preparation of the surface coupons shall be undertaken through the inoculation of each
   coupon with a defined quantity of the suspension of the challenge microorganism, and the
   drying of the inoculum shall be permitted to proceed for a period of time that is deemed to
   be sufficient.
2. The application of the disinfectant to the inoculated coupons shall be performed for the
   contact time that is under evaluation, and the recovery of the surviving microorganisms
   shall be accomplished through the transfer of each coupon to a suitable diluent that
   contains the appropriate neutralizing agent.
3. The performance of the enumeration of the surviving microorganisms shall be carried out in
   accordance with SOP-QC-001, and the calculation of the logarithmic reduction shall be
   accomplished through the comparison of the recovery from the treated coupons against the
   recovery from the corresponding untreated control coupons.
4. The conduct of a confirmation of the effectiveness of the neutralization shall be undertaken
   through the demonstration that the neutralizing agent provides the inactivation of the
   residual disinfectant without the exertion of an inhibitory effect upon the recovery of the
   challenge microorganisms.
5. The provision of documentation of the results shall be accomplished in accordance with
   SOP-DOC-001 and the environmental relevance of the qualification shall be supported through
   the correlation of the results with the environmental monitoring data that are generated in
   accordance with SOP-ENV-001.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

The disinfectant shall be considered to have been subject to a successful qualification when
the achievement of the required logarithmic reduction of the populations of the challenge
microorganisms is demonstrated for the contact time that is under evaluation, and any result
for which the demonstration of the required reduction is not accomplished shall be subject to
an investigation and the performance of a re-qualification shall be undertaken as required.

RELATED DOCUMENTS AND REFERENCES

This procedure shall be read in conjunction with SOP-CLN-006, SOP-QC-001, and SOP-ENV-001. The
qualification of the associated analytical instrumentation is maintained in accordance with
USP <1058>.
