## Purpose

This procedure covers storage of temperature-sensitive materials and finished product. It also covers the management of temperature excursions.

## Scope

Applies to refrigerated (2–8 °C) and frozen (−20 °C) storage at Building 4.

## Responsibility

Cold chain units should be monitored by warehouse staff. The Warehouse Manager should ensure that alarms are responded to as necessary. Quality Assurance should disposition affected material.

## Storage

Product should be stored within the labeled temperature range. Refrigerated material should be held at 2–8 °C. Frozen material should be held at or below −20 °C. Units should not be overloaded, and airflow should be kept adequate.

## Monitoring

Temperature is recorded continuously by a calibrated monitoring system. Readings are reviewed at a suitable frequency. Alarms are configured for excursions outside the acceptable range.

## Excursion Management

- When an excursion is detected, affected product should be quarantined in a timely manner.
- The event is recorded, and the duration and magnitude of the excursion are documented.
- Affected material is placed on hold and is not distributed until it has been dispositioned.
- The impact is assessed by Quality Assurance as required, and mean kinetic temperature is taken into account where appropriate.
- Material received under quarantine is handled in accordance with SOP-WHS-001.

## Records

Records are completed and reviewed in accordance with SOP-DOC-001.
