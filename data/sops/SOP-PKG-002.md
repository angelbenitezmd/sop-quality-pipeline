## Purpose

- This procedure describes the secondary packaging and labeling of finished vial product at Building 4.
- The Packaging & Labeling function is responsible for ensuring that vial product is cartoned, labeled, and case-packed in a manner that maintains product identity and traceability.

## Scope

- This procedure applies to the secondary packaging of filled, inspected, and released injectable vials.
- It applies to cartoning, carton labeling, leaflet insertion, and case packing performed on the secondary packaging line.

## Responsibilities

- The Packaging Supervisor is responsible for ensuring that the line is set up against an approved packaging order and that personnel are trained as required.
- The Line Operator is responsible for ensuring that vials, cartons, and leaflets are loaded correctly and that in-process checks are completed as necessary.
- The Line Clearance Checker is responsible for ensuring that the line is cleared of product and components from the previous batch before setup.
- Quality Assurance is responsible for ensuring that reconciliation and in-process results are reviewed prior to disposition.

## Materials and Components

- Filled and inspected vials bearing an approved primary label
- Printed cartons and package leaflets issued in accordance with SOP-PKG-001
- Shipper cases and case labels
- Verified check-weigh station (as required)

## Line Setup and Line Clearance

- Before packaging is started, line clearance is performed so that no vials, cartons, labels, or leaflets from a prior batch remain on or around the line.
- The packaging order, cartons, and leaflets are verified against the approved specimen to confirm that the correct components have been issued.
- The lot number and expiry date are checked to confirm that they are legible and correct.

## Secondary Packaging Procedure

- Vials are loaded into the cartoning module and combined with the appropriate leaflet.
- Each carton is filled with the defined number of vials (10 vials per carton) and closed.
- Cartons are conveyed to the labeling station, where the carton label and any variable data are applied.
- Cartons are case-packed into shippers at 12 cartons per shipper, and the case label is applied.
- Any reject cartons are segregated in a suitable manner and reconciled as waste.

## Labeling and Reconciliation

- Carton labels and case labels are reconciled against the quantity issued in accordance with SOP-PKG-001.
- Reconciliation is documented in the batch record in a timely manner, and any discrepancy considered significant is investigated as required.
- The Packaging Supervisor is responsible for ensuring that reconciliation is completed and verified before the batch is closed.

## In-Process Checks

- At an appropriate frequency, cartons are checked for correct leaflet presence, correct label placement, and legible printing.
- Check-weighing is performed periodically to confirm that carton contents are adequate.
- Results are recorded and any nonconforming unit is removed as necessary.
