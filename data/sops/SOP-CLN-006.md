PURPOSE

This procedure describes how the sporicidal agent shall be prepared and rotated for use in the
classified cleanrooms of Building 4. It exists so that a broad spectrum of microbial control
is maintained and so that the emergence of resistant microbial populations is limited.

SCOPE

This procedure shall be applied to the preparation and rotation of the sporicidal agent that
is used in the routine disinfection of the ISO 7 and ISO 8 cleanrooms. It shall be used
together with the routine cleaning activities described in SOP-CLN-001.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Sanitization personnel shall prepare and apply the sporicidal agent as described in this
procedure. The area supervisor shall verify that the rotation schedule is followed and that
each preparation is documented.

MATERIALS

The following shall be used: the concentrated sporicidal agent, Water-for-Injection or purified
water as specified on the product label, a graduated container, and personal protective
equipment. Only sporicidal agent that is within its labeled shelf life shall be used.

PREPARATION

1. The sporicidal agent shall be diluted to the concentration stated on the approved product
   label.
2. The prepared solution shall be labeled with the preparation date, the expiry time, and the
   initials of the preparer.
3. The prepared solution shall be used within the in-use expiry period stated on the label and
   shall be discarded when that period is exceeded.

ROTATION SCHEDULE

1. The sporicidal agent shall be applied on a weekly basis and after any event that requires
   enhanced disinfection.
2. The sporicidal agent shall be rotated with the routine disinfectant so that both agents are
   used within each monthly cycle.
3. The rotation record shall be maintained by the Contamination Control function.

ENVIRONMENTAL VERIFICATION

Environmental monitoring shall be performed after sporicidal application in accordance with
SOP-ENV-001 so that the effectiveness of the disinfection is confirmed.

RELATED DOCUMENTS AND REFERENCES

This procedure shall be read together with SOP-CLN-001 and SOP-ENV-001. The control of
microbiological contamination is maintained in accordance with 21 CFR 211.113.
