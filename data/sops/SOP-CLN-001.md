PURPOSE AND APPLICABILITY

The purpose of this standard operating procedure is to describe the manual cleaning and
disinfection activities that shall be performed within the ISO 7 classified cleanrooms of
Building 4 in order that an appropriate and adequate state of control with respect to
microbial and particulate contamination is maintained at all times during and between
manufacturing campaigns, and it shall be understood that the activities described herein
shall be performed only by trained sanitization personnel who have been suitably qualified
in accordance with the site training program and who have been deemed competent by the area
supervisor as required.

SCOPE

This procedure shall be applied to the routine and periodic cleaning and disinfection of all
ISO 7 classified cleanroom surfaces, including but not limited to floors, walls, ceilings,
doors, pass-through chambers, work surfaces, and the accessible exterior surfaces of
equipment, and it shall be applied as necessary and as frequently as is deemed to be
appropriate by the area supervisor in accordance with the established cleaning frequency
matrix. The cleaning of product contact vessels and transfer lines shall be considered to be
outside the scope of this procedure and shall be performed in accordance with SOP-CLN-002.

RESPONSIBILITIES

It is the responsibility of sanitization personnel that the cleaning and disinfection of the
ISO 7 cleanrooms shall be carried out in the manner that is described within this procedure,
and it is the responsibility of the area supervisor that adequate oversight and verification
of the activities shall be provided as required, and it is further the responsibility of the
Contamination Control function that this procedure shall be maintained in a current state and
that the personnel who perform the activities shall be trained in a timely manner.

MATERIALS AND EQUIPMENT

The following materials shall be utilized: 70% isopropyl alcohol (IPA), a sporicidal agent
that shall be prepared and rotated in accordance with the applicable sporicidal rotation
schedule, low-particulate wipes, and a validated mopping system with suitable buckets. The
concentration of isopropyl alcohol that shall be used is 70% and the contact time that shall
be observed is 0.5 minutes minimum for the isopropyl alcohol and 5.0 minutes minimum for the
sporicidal agent, and it shall be ensured that the surfaces are permitted to remain visibly
wet for a period of time that is sufficient to achieve the required contact time.

CLEANING AND DISINFECTION PROCEDURE

1. Prior to entry, gowning shall be performed in accordance with the applicable gowning
   procedure and the cleanroom shall be inspected in order that it is confirmed to be
   suitable for cleaning and free of any obvious spillage or debris.
2. Cleaning shall be commenced at the cleanest point of the cleanroom and shall be progressed
   in an orderly fashion toward the least clean point, and it shall be ensured throughout that
   a systematic and overlapping stroke pattern is being followed so that no area is omitted.
3. All work surfaces and accessible equipment exteriors shall be wiped with 70% isopropyl
   alcohol and it shall be ensured that a contact time of not less than 0.5 minutes is
   achieved before the surface is permitted to dry.
4. On a periodic basis, and as necessary in accordance with the rotation schedule, the
   sporicidal agent shall be applied to the surfaces of the cleanroom and a contact time of
   not less than 5.0 minutes shall be observed, after which any residue shall be removed as
   appropriate with 70% isopropyl alcohol.
5. Floors within the cleanroom shall be mopped using the validated mopping system and the
   mopping shall be performed in a manner that is appropriate, and the mop heads shall be
   changed at a frequency that is deemed to be sufficient.
6. Upon completion, the cleaning and disinfection activities shall be documented in the
   cleaning logbook and the records shall be reviewed in accordance with SOP-DOC-001.

SPORICIDAL AGENT ROTATION

The sporicidal agent shall be rotated with the routine disinfectant on a defined schedule in
order that the development of resistant microbial populations is prevented, and the rotation
shall be performed periodically and as required in accordance with the established rotation
matrix that is maintained by the Contamination Control function.

ENVIRONMENTAL VERIFICATION

Following cleaning and disinfection, environmental monitoring shall be performed in
accordance with SOP-ENV-001 in order that the effectiveness of the cleaning and disinfection
is verified, and any result that is found to exceed the applicable action level shall be
investigated in a timely manner as required.

RELATED DOCUMENTS AND REFERENCES

This procedure shall be read in conjunction with SOP-CLN-002, SOP-ENV-001, and SOP-DOC-001.
Cleaning and disinfection activities shall be conducted in accordance with the applicable
requirements of 21 CFR 211.67.
