## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines the good documentation practices (GDP) and data integrity
requirements that apply to every GMP record generated at Building 4. It exists so that
each record is attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original, and accurate.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all paper and electronic GMP records, including batch records,
logbooks, cleaning records, environmental monitoring data, and laboratory results.

## 3. Responsibilities

Each employee who creates or reviews a GMP record must follow this procedure. Area
supervisors must verify that records are completed correctly before batch disposition.
Quality Systems must maintain the controlled document library.

## 4. ALCOA+ Principles

1. Record data at the time you perform the activity.
2. Sign and date every entry using your assigned credentials.
3. Write legibly in indelible ink; do not use pencil.
4. Correct an error with a single line strike-through, then initial and date the change.
5. Never obscure the original entry.
6. Do not backdate or pre-date any record.

## 5. Electronic Records

1. Log in with your unique user account before entering data.
2. Do not share passwords or leave a session unattended.
3. Confirm that the system applies an audit trail to every change.
4. Review the audit trail during batch record review.

## 6. Correcting Errors

1. Draw a single line through the incorrect entry.
2. Write the correct value adjacent to the strike-through.
3. Initial and date the correction.
4. Add a brief reason code when the change affects a result or a decision.
