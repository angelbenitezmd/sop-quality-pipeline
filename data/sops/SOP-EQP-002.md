## Purpose

This procedure describes how moist-heat sterilization loads should be prepared, configured, and processed in Autoclave A (Asset EQP-AUT-A), a saturated-steam porous-load sterilizer used for the sterilization of component hardware, garments, and equipment parts. The validated load patterns are to be followed exactly so that the sterilization of every item in the chamber is assured.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all sterilization cycles that are run on Autoclave A. Only the load configurations that have been qualified for Autoclave A should be processed, and any new configuration should be qualified before it is placed into routine use.

## Responsibilities

Cycles on Autoclave A should be operated by qualified sterilization technicians. Load release should be reviewed by the area supervisor. Cycle qualification and requalification should be maintained by Engineering.

## Equipment and Materials

- Autoclave A (EQP-AUT-A), saturated-steam porous-load sterilizer
- Chamber load carts and validated load templates for Autoclave A
- Chemical indicators and biological indicators handled per SOP-QC-005
- Sterilization-grade wrap and perforated trays

## Procedure

Step 1: Before a cycle is started, the qualification and calibration status of Autoclave A should be confirmed by the operator, and the equipment identification EQP-AUT-A should be recorded in the sterilization log. A cycle should not be started whenever a chamber fault or an open maintenance record is present.

Step 2: Items should be prepared, wrapped, and arranged on the load cart in accordance with the qualified load pattern for Autoclave A, and it should be ensured by the operator that dense items are placed on lower shelves and that no item is allowed to contact the chamber wall.

Step 3: Chemical indicators should be applied to representative packs and, where the cycle requires it, biological indicators should be positioned at the qualified worst-case locations. Biological indicators should be reconciled and incubated in accordance with SOP-QC-005.

Step 4: The correct validated cycle should be selected for Autoclave A. For the porous-load cycle, an exposure temperature of 121 °C and an exposure time of 30 minutes should be applied, and the chamber pressure and a successful air-removal (vacuum leak) test should be confirmed before exposure is permitted to begin.

Step 5: The cycle should be monitored until completion, and the printed cycle record should be checked so that the exposure temperature, exposure time, and F0 are confirmed to meet the acceptance criteria. Any cycle in which the parameters are not met should be rejected and a deviation should be raised.

Step 6: On completion, the load should be unloaded, the chemical indicators should be read, and the sterilized-status label should be applied. The records should be reviewed and dispositioned by the area supervisor before the load is released for use.

## Acceptance Criteria

A cycle should be accepted only when the exposure temperature is at or above 121 °C for the full 30-minute exposure, the calculated F0 is at least 15 minutes, all chemical indicators have changed correctly, and any biological indicators subsequently show no growth per SOP-QC-005.
