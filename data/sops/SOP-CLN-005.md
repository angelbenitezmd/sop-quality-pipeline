PURPOSE AND APPLICABILITY

The purpose of this standard operating procedure is to describe the manual cleaning and
disinfection activities that shall be performed within Cleanroom C, which is the ISO 7
gowning and airlock area, in order to ensure that a state of control with respect to microbial
and particulate contamination is maintained at all times during and between manufacturing
campaigns, and it is understood that the activities described herein shall be performed by
trained sanitization personnel who have been appropriately qualified in accordance with the
site training program.

SCOPE

This procedure shall be applied to all cleaning of Cleanroom C surfaces, including floors,
walls, work surfaces, the interior surfaces of the airlock and the pass-through hatches that
serve the suite, and the exterior of equipment, and it shall be applied as necessary
whenever cleaning is deemed to be appropriate by the area supervisor.

RESPONSIBILITIES

It is the responsibility of sanitization personnel that the cleaning of Cleanroom C shall
be carried out in the manner that is described within this procedure, and it is the
responsibility of the area supervisor that adequate oversight is provided as required.

MATERIALS AND EQUIPMENT

The following materials shall be utilized: 70% isopropyl alcohol (IPA), a sporicidal agent
prepared in accordance with SOP-CLN-006, low-particulate wipes, and a validated mopping
system. The concentration of isopropyl alcohol that shall be used is 70% and the contact
time that shall be observed is 0.5 minutes minimum for IPA and 5 minutes for the sporicidal
agent.

CLEANING PROCEDURE

1. Prior to entry, gowning shall be performed in accordance with the applicable gowning
   procedure and the area shall be inspected to confirm that it is suitable for cleaning.
2. Cleaning shall be commenced at the cleanest point of Cleanroom C and shall be progressed
   toward the least clean point, and it shall be ensured that a systematic and overlapping
   stroke pattern is being followed.
3. All work surfaces within Cleanroom C shall be wiped with 70% isopropyl alcohol and it
   shall be ensured that the surfaces are allowed to remain wet for a period of time that is
   sufficient to achieve the required contact time.
4. On a periodic basis, and as necessary, the sporicidal agent shall be applied to the
   surfaces of Cleanroom C in accordance with the rotation schedule.
5. Floors within Cleanroom C shall be mopped using the validated mopping system and the
   mopping shall be performed in a manner that is appropriate.
6. Upon completion, the cleaning activities shall be documented in the cleaning logbook and
   the records shall be reviewed in accordance with SOP-DOC-001.

ENVIRONMENTAL VERIFICATION

Following cleaning, environmental monitoring shall be performed in accordance with
SOP-ENV-002 in order that the effectiveness of the cleaning is verified.

RELATED DOCUMENTS AND REFERENCES

This procedure shall be read in conjunction with SOP-CLN-001 and SOP-ENV-002. The cleaning
program is maintained in accordance with 21 CFR 211.67.
