## Purpose

This procedure covers receipt, identification, and quarantine of incoming materials. It applies to raw materials, primary components, and printed packaging components.

## Scope

All incoming GMP materials received at the Building 4 warehouse.

## Responsibility

Warehouse staff should receive and quarantine materials. The Warehouse Manager should ensure staff are trained as required. Quality Assurance should release or reject materials.

## Procedure

1. Check the delivery against the purchase order on receipt. Confirm the material name and quantity are correct.
2. Inspect each container for damage. Reject any container that is not suitable.
3. Verify received quantities as necessary. Where quantity is confirmed by weight, use a calibrated balance in accordance with SOP-QC-006.
4. Assign a unique receipt number. Record it in the receiving log.
5. Apply a QUARANTINE status label to each container.
6. Move the materials to the quarantine area in a timely manner.
7. Materials should remain in quarantine until released by Quality Assurance.
8. Sampling should be performed as required before release.
9. Released materials should be moved to the approved storage area. Rejected materials should be segregated and held pending disposition.

## Storage

Store materials under suitable conditions. Keep quarantine and released materials separate at all times.

## Records

Complete and review all records in accordance with SOP-DOC-001.
