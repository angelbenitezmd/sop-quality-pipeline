## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines the change control and deviation management systems at Building 4.
It exists so that the evaluation of proposed changes and the investigation of deviations
follow a controlled path.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to every proposed change to a facility, utility, equipment,
process, or document. It also applies to every planned and unplanned deviation from an
approved procedure.

## 3. Responsibilities

The initiator must provide documentation of the proposed change or deviation. The area
owner must support the performance of an impact assessment. Quality Assurance must
approve the closure of each record.

## 4. Change Classification

1. Assign each change a classification of minor, major, or critical.
2. Base the classification on the outcome of a risk assessment.
3. Apply the quality risk management principles of ICH Q9(R1).
4. Record the justification for the assigned classification.

## 5. Change Control Process

1. Initiate a change control record for the proposed change.
2. Complete an assessment of the impact on product quality and validated status.
3. Provide documentation of the required actions and approvals.
4. Obtain approval from Quality Assurance before implementation of the change.
5. Confirm the completion of every action prior to closure of the record.

## 6. Deviation Management

1. Initiate a deviation record at the time of discovery of the event.
2. Perform an assessment of the impact on any affected batch.
3. Conduct an investigation into the root cause of the deviation.
4. Support the disposition of the batch with a documented rationale.
5. Ensure the completion of the production record review under 21 CFR 211.192.

## 7. CAPA and Effectiveness

1. Define corrective and preventive actions for each confirmed root cause.
2. Assign an owner and a due date to each action.
3. Perform verification of the effectiveness of every closed action.
4. Escalate a repeat deviation to the quality management review.

## 8. Documentation

1. Record each change and deviation in the quality system.
2. Manage every document change through SOP-DOC-002.
3. Maintain each record per SOP-DOC-001.
4. Retain the closed records per the site retention schedule.
