## Purpose

This procedure provides for the description of the setup, operation, and post-cycle handling of the production lyophilizer (Asset EQP-LYO-01) in a manner that establishes the assurance of the reproducible execution of the freezing, primary drying, and secondary drying phases of the qualified lyophilization cycle, and it is the intention of this procedure that, through the provision of a consistent methodology for the configuration of the equipment and for the verification of the readiness of the equipment prior to loading, the achievement of the required attributes of the finished lyophilized product with respect to residual moisture, cake appearance, and reconstitution behaviour should be supported in a state of demonstrable control.

## Scope

This procedure is applicable to the performance of the operation of the production lyophilizer for all qualified product cycles, inclusive of the loading of partially stoppered vials, the execution of the automated recipe, the accomplishment of stoppering under the specified chamber condition, and the conduct of the post-cycle defrost and cleaning, and it is applicable to the senior process engineers and to the qualified operators to whom the responsibility for the conduct of these activities has been assigned.

## Responsibilities

The performance of the operation of the lyophilizer should be undertaken by qualified operators; the provision of technical oversight of the recipe and of any investigation of an excursion should be undertaken by the senior process engineer; and the maintenance of the qualification status of the equipment and of the associated automation should be undertaken by Engineering.

## Equipment and Materials

- Production lyophilizer EQP-LYO-01 with shelf stack, condenser, and hydraulic stoppering system
- Programmable recipe controller with electronic batch record capability
- Qualified thermocouples and capacitance-manometer / Pirani comparison for endpoint determination
- Sterilized, partially stoppered vials transferred from the filling line per SOP-EQP-001

## Procedure

Step 1: Prior to the initiation of any cycle, the operator should perform the verification of the qualification and calibration status of the lyophilizer and of its shelf-temperature and chamber-pressure instrumentation, and, in recognition of the fact that the reliability of the endpoint determination is entirely dependent upon the integrity of that instrumentation, the operator should provide documentation of the completion of the pre-cycle checks, inclusive of the confirmation of the achievement of the shelf-temperature setpoint, the confirmation of the achievement of condenser readiness, and the confirmation of the successful completion of the most recent chamber leak-rate assessment, before the authorization of loading is granted.

Step 2: The loading of the partially stoppered vials onto the shelves should be conducted in accordance with the qualified loading pattern in such a way that the assurance of the uniformity of the thermal condition across the shelf stack is provided, and, throughout the accomplishment of the loading operation, the maintenance of the aseptic condition of the load and the minimization of the duration of the exposure of the load should be given priority, with the coordination of the upstream stopper processing being maintained in accordance with SOP-MFG-005.

Step 3: The selection and initiation of the qualified recipe should be performed by the operator, and it should be understood that the freezing phase, through the establishment of the required shelf temperature and the provision of an adequate holding period, effects the accomplishment of the complete solidification of the formulation, which is a prerequisite for the avoidance of the collapse of the cake during the subsequent reduction of the chamber pressure and the commencement of primary drying.

Step 4: During the conduct of primary drying, the reduction of the chamber pressure to the recipe setpoint and the controlled elevation of the shelf temperature should be monitored continuously, and the determination of the endpoint of primary drying should be made through the performance of an evaluation of the convergence of the capacitance-manometer and Pirani pressure readings together with the assessment of the product-temperature profile, since the premature advancement to secondary drying, in the event that a portion of the load has not achieved the completion of sublimation, constitutes the principal risk to the attainment of the residual-moisture specification.

Step 5: Upon the completion of secondary drying and the achievement of the target residual-moisture condition, the accomplishment of stoppering should be effected through the actuation of the hydraulic system under the specified chamber atmosphere and pressure, and the provision of documentation of the stoppering condition and of the associated chamber parameters should be ensured within the electronic batch record.

Step 6: Following the completion of the cycle, the performance of the unloading, the visual assessment of cake appearance, and the initiation of the defrost and cleaning of the chamber and condenser should be conducted, and the review of the electronic batch record and the associated cycle trends should be undertaken in a manner that provides for the confirmation that every phase parameter remained within the qualified acceptance range.

## Acceptance Criteria

A cycle should be considered acceptable only upon the demonstration that the freezing, primary-drying, and secondary-drying parameters remained within the qualified ranges, that the residual-moisture result met its specification, and that the assessment of cake appearance and reconstitution provided no evidence of collapse or of incomplete drying; any deviation from these conditions should result in the placement of the affected units on hold and the initiation of an investigation.

## Computerized System Controls

The recipe-management and electronic-batch-record functions of the lyophilizer automation should be subjected to the maintenance of a validated state through the application of the risk-based, life-cycle methodology set out in GAMP 5 (1st Ed), inclusive of the provision of documentation of the qualification of the system and the assurance of the completion of the periodic review of the audit-trail configuration.
