I. Purpose

This procedure defines how the laboratory calibrates and verifies analytical balances. It
ensures that every weighing used for a GMP result is accurate and traceable.

II. Scope

This procedure applies to all analytical balances in the QC laboratory. It covers the annual
external calibration and the daily verification performed by the analyst before use.

III. Responsibilities

The QC analyst must perform the daily verification and record the result. The QC Laboratory
Manager must schedule the annual external calibration by a qualified service provider. The
analyst must not use a balance that fails verification.

IV. Materials and Equipment

- A set of calibrated reference weights traceable to a national standard
- Certificates of calibration for the reference weights
- The balance logbook and lint-free gloves or forceps for handling weights

V. Daily Verification

1. Confirm that the balance is level and clean, and allow it to warm up as needed.
2. Handle reference weights with forceps or gloves; never touch them by hand.
3. Place a low-range and a high-range reference weight on the pan, one at a time.
4. Record each displayed value in the balance logbook.
5. Confirm that each reading is within ±0.1% of the nominal weight value.
6. If a reading is out of tolerance, remove the balance from service and notify the QC
   Laboratory Manager.

VI. Annual Calibration

The QC Laboratory Manager must arrange an annual calibration by a qualified external service
provider. The provider must supply a calibration certificate traceable to a national
standard. The balance must remain qualified in line with USP <1058>.

VII. Acceptance Criteria

- Daily verification: each reading is within ±0.1% of the nominal value.
- Annual calibration: the balance meets the tolerances stated on the calibration certificate.

VIII. Documentation

Record every verification and calibration in the balance logbook. Complete all entries
following SOP-DOC-001, and file the annual calibration certificate with the equipment
records.
