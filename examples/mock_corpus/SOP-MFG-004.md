## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how the operator will perform the execution of the aseptic process
simulation, commonly designated the media fill, so that the demonstration of the capability of
the aseptic filling process to maintain sterility will be provided, and so that the
qualification of operators, the evaluation of interventions, and the confirmation of the state
of control of the filling line will be documented in a manner that supports the ongoing
verification of the aseptic manufacturing operations described in SOP-MFG-001.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to the performance of all aseptic process simulations conducted on the
vial and syringe filling lines, and it will be applied to the initial qualification of a line,
to the periodic requalification of a line at the frequency defined in the validation master
plan, and to any requalification that becomes necessary following a significant change,
because the maintenance of the qualified status of each line depends upon the successful
completion of these simulations at the required intervals.

## 3. Responsibilities

The operator will conduct the performance of the media fill in the same manner in which the
routine filling operation is performed, and the operator will make no deviation from routine
practice other than the substitution of the sterile growth medium for product; the line lead
will provide the coordination of the run and the documentation of every intervention; and the
Quality Unit will provide the review of the results and the disposition of the simulation
batch, so that the assurance of an independent assessment of the outcome will be maintained.

## 4. Media Fill Design

The design of the simulation will be established such that the representation of the worst-case
conditions of the routine process will be achieved, and, in the establishment of that design,
the operator will ensure the inclusion of the maximum permitted duration of filling, the
maximum number of personnel, and the incorporation of the full range of inherent and
corrective interventions that could occur during a routine campaign, because the credibility
of the simulation is dependent upon the achievement of a realistic and challenging
representation of production. The determination of the number of units to be filled will be
made such that a sufficient quantity is produced to allow the detection of a low incidence of
contamination.

## 5. Materials and Equipment

The operator will use a sterile, filterable microbiological growth medium, typically
soybean-casein digest medium, together with the same filling line, the same change parts, and
the same container-closure system that are used for routine production. The preparation of the
medium and the confirmation of its growth-promotion capability will be performed prior to the
run, and the retention of a portion of the medium for growth-promotion testing after
incubation will be ensured.

## 6. Procedure

1. Line clearance will be verified and the batch record for the simulation will be reviewed
   before the introduction of any component, and the confirmation of the readiness of the line
   will be documented.
2. The operator will perform the setup of the line and the disinfection of Grade A surfaces in
   the manner described for routine filling, and the confirmation that environmental monitoring
   is being performed in accordance with SOP-ENV-001 will be obtained.
3. The medium will be filtered through the sterilizing-grade filter and the filling of
   containers will be commenced, and, throughout the run, the operator will introduce the
   planned interventions at the points at which their occurrence would be expected during a
   routine campaign.
4. Environmental and personnel monitoring samples are collected during the simulation and the
   submission of those samples for the enumeration of recovered organisms will be made.
5. Upon completion, the containers are stoppered, the reconciliation of filled units will be
   performed, and the transfer of the units to incubation will be documented.

## 7. Incubation and Reading

The incubation of the filled units will be performed at the defined temperatures for the
defined durations, and the performance of an inspection of every unit for evidence of
microbial growth will be conducted by qualified personnel at the intervals specified. Any unit
that exhibits turbidity will be subjected to the performance of an investigation, and the
identification of the recovered organism will be obtained so that the correlation of the
finding with environmental and sterility data generated under SOP-QC-002 can be assessed.

## 8. Acceptance Criteria

The evaluation of the outcome will be made against a zero-growth expectation, and the
determination that the simulation is acceptable will be made when the number of contaminated
units meets the acceptance criterion defined in the validation master plan; in the event that
the criterion is not met, the invalidation of the qualified status of the line will result and
the performance of an investigation and the completion of a requalification will be required
before routine filling is resumed.
