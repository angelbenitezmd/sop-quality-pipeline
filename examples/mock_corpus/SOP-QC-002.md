I. Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is the definition of the methodology for the performance of
sterility testing of sterile injectable drug products through the technique of membrane
filtration, such that the demonstration of the absence of viable microbial contamination is
provided in support of the disposition of the associated batches.

II. Scope

The applicability of this procedure extends to the conduct of sterility testing of finished
drug product, of media-fill units that are generated during the performance of aseptic
process simulation, and of any additional articles for which the demonstration of sterility
is required. The performance of testing of media-fill units must be conducted in
coordination with the aseptic process simulation program that is described in SOP-MFG-004.

III. Responsibilities

The QC microbiology analyst must undertake the performance of the test within the sterility
isolator, the preparation of the media, and the documentation of all observations in
accordance with SOP-DOC-001. The QC reviewer must conduct an assessment of the completeness
of the record prior to the approval of the result. The QC Laboratory Manager must ensure the
maintenance of the qualified status of the personnel and of the sterility isolator.

IV. Materials, Media, and Equipment

The following must be available for the conduct of the test: a unidirectional-airflow
sterility isolator; a closed canister membrane-filtration apparatus fitted with filters of
0.45-micrometre nominal pore size; Fluid Thioglycollate Medium for the detection of
anaerobic and aerobic microorganisms; Soybean–Casein Digest Medium for the detection of
aerobic microorganisms and fungi; sterile rinsing fluid; and negative and positive controls.
The confirmation of the growth-promotion capability of each lot of medium must be provided
prior to use, and the incubation of biological indicators that is described in SOP-QC-005
must not be conducted within the same incubators that are designated for sterility-test
media.

V. Procedure

1. The disinfection of the isolator and the transfer of all articles into the isolator must
   be performed in accordance with the established transfer methodology.
2. The performance of the filtration must be undertaken through the aseptic transfer of the
   product into the canisters, the passage of the product through the membrane, and the
   subsequent rinsing of the membrane with the prescribed volume of sterile rinsing fluid.
3. The distribution of the media into the canisters must be performed such that one canister
   receives Fluid Thioglycollate Medium and the second canister receives Soybean–Casein
   Digest Medium.
4. The incubation of the Fluid Thioglycollate Medium must be conducted at 30–35 °C and the
   incubation of the Soybean–Casein Digest Medium must be conducted at 20–25 °C, and the
   maintenance of the incubation must be provided for a period of not fewer than 14 days.
5. The performance of an observation of the canisters for evidence of turbidity or other
   indication of microbial growth must be undertaken at intervals during the incubation
   period and at the conclusion of the incubation period.

VI. Interpretation and Acceptance Criteria

The interpretation of the test must be performed in accordance with USP <71>. The absence
of the appearance of microbial growth in all test canisters, together with the demonstration
of the validity of the associated controls, is considered to represent a conforming outcome.
In the event of the observation of growth, the performance of an investigation must be
undertaken for the determination of whether the growth is attributable to a cause that is
not related to the product, and the retest provisions that are described in USP <71> must be
applied only where the establishment of a laboratory cause has been documented.

VII. Data Review and Documentation

The documentation of all observations, of all incubation temperatures, and of all control
outcomes must be maintained, and the performance of a second-person review must be
undertaken prior to the approval of the result in accordance with SOP-DOC-001.
