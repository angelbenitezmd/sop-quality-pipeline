PURPOSE AND APPLICABILITY

The purpose of this standard operating procedure is to describe the automated clean-in-place
activities that shall be performed on the product contact vessels, mixing tanks, and transfer
lines of Building 4 in order that product residues, cleaning agent residues, and microbial
bioburden are removed to levels that are below the established acceptance limits, and it shall
be understood that the clean-in-place cycle described herein shall be executed only after the
associated equipment has been placed into a condition that is suitable for cleaning and only
by personnel who have been trained in the operation of the clean-in-place skid in accordance
with the site training program.

SCOPE

This procedure shall be applied to the clean-in-place of all product contact surfaces of the
mixing vessels, the holding vessels, and the associated transfer lines and valves that are
served by the central clean-in-place skid, and it shall be applied following the completion
of a manufacturing campaign, following a product changeover, and at any other time at which
the cleaning of the equipment is determined to be required.

RESPONSIBILITIES

It is the responsibility of the operating personnel that the clean-in-place cycle shall be
initiated, monitored, and documented in accordance with this procedure, and it is the
responsibility of the area supervisor that the acceptability of the cycle results shall be
verified prior to the release of the equipment for subsequent use, and it is the
responsibility of the Quality Control function that the analytical testing of the final rinse
samples shall be performed in accordance with SOP-QC-004.

MATERIALS AND EQUIPMENT

The following shall be utilized: Water-for-Injection (WFI) supplied in accordance with
SOP-EQP-004, an alkaline cleaning agent, an acid cleaning agent where the soil profile is
determined to warrant its use, and the central clean-in-place skid together with its
associated spray devices, and it shall be ensured that the cleaning agents are prepared at
the concentrations that are specified within the applicable cycle recipe and that the
temperatures and flow rates are maintained within the ranges that are defined for the vessel
that is being cleaned.

CLEANING PROCEDURE

1. A pre-rinse shall be performed with Water-for-Injection in order that the bulk of the gross
   product soil is removed from the product contact surfaces prior to the introduction of the
   cleaning agent, and the pre-rinse shall be continued until the rinse effluent is observed
   to be substantially free of visible soil.
2. An alkaline wash shall be circulated through the vessel and the associated transfer lines
   at the temperature, flow rate, and duration that are defined within the applicable cycle
   recipe, and it shall be ensured that all product contact surfaces, including the spray
   shadow areas, are contacted by the cleaning solution throughout the wash phase.
3. Where the soil profile is determined to require it, an acid wash shall be circulated in the
   same manner as the alkaline wash in order that inorganic residues are removed.
4. An intermediate rinse and a final rinse shall be performed with Water-for-Injection in
   order that the cleaning agent residues are removed from the product contact surfaces to a
   level that is below the established acceptance limit.
5. A final-rinse sample shall be collected from the defined sampling point and shall be
   submitted for the determination of conductivity and total organic carbon in accordance with
   SOP-QC-004.
6. Upon the completion of the cycle, the clean-in-place records shall be reviewed and approved
   in accordance with SOP-DOC-001 prior to the release of the equipment.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

The clean-in-place cycle shall be considered to be acceptable when the final-rinse conductivity
is not greater than 1.3 microsiemens per centimeter and the final-rinse total organic carbon
is not greater than 500 parts per billion, and any result that is found to exceed these limits
shall be investigated and the cycle shall be repeated as required.

RELATED DOCUMENTS AND REFERENCES

This procedure shall be read in conjunction with SOP-EQP-004, SOP-QC-004, and SOP-DOC-001.
The clean-in-place program is maintained in accordance with
ISPE Baseline Guide: CIP (2nd Ed, 2020) and PDA TR-70.
