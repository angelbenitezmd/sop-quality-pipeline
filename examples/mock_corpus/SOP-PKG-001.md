## Purpose

- This procedure defines the controls for the issuance and reconciliation of printed labels used on drug product at Building 4.
- The Packaging & Labeling function is responsible for ensuring that only correct, approved labels are issued to a packaging line and that all issued labels are reconciled after use.

## Scope

- This procedure applies to all primary and secondary labels, cartons, and printed packaging components that bear product-identifying information.
- It applies to vial and pre-filled syringe product packaged on Lines 1 and 2.

## Responsibilities

- The Packaging Supervisor is responsible for ensuring that label issuance is authorized against an approved and effective packaging order and that trained personnel are assigned as required.
- The Label Issuance Coordinator is responsible for ensuring that labels are drawn from the controlled label store, counted, and reconciled in a timely manner.
- The Line Operator is responsible for ensuring that labels received at the line are verified against the batch record before use.
- Quality Assurance is responsible for ensuring that reconciliation discrepancies are reviewed and dispositioned as appropriate.

## Materials

- Approved label specimen from the controlled artwork file
- Controlled label store issue log
- Verified balance or automated label counter (as necessary)

## Label Issuance

- Labels are issued only against an approved and effective packaging order.
- The required quantity is calculated from the batch size plus an appropriate overage for setup and in-process verification.
- Issued labels are compared against the approved specimen so that the text, lot number, and expiry format are confirmed to be suitable.
- Each issuance is recorded in the issue log with the label item number, the quantity issued, and the signature of the coordinator.
- Where labels for more than one batch are handled, the labels are segregated so that mix-up is prevented.

## Reconciliation

- At the completion of packaging, used, unused, and defective/waste labels are reconciled against the quantity that was issued.
- Reconciliation is performed and documented in a timely manner, and any discrepancy outside the acceptable range is investigated as required.
- Excess or obsolete labels are returned to the controlled store or destroyed as appropriate, and the destruction is documented.
- The Packaging Supervisor is responsible for ensuring that reconciliation is verified before the batch is released to the next step.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Label reconciliation is required to balance within the limits defined in the batch record.
- A variance that is considered significant is escalated to Quality Assurance for review before disposition.
