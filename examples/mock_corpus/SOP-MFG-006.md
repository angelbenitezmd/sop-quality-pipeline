## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how the operator will perform the in-process controls that are
applied during aseptic filling so that fill weight, container closure, and product appearance
will be verified throughout each batch. It exists so that the process will be confirmed to
remain within its validated limits and so that deviations will be detected promptly.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all in-process controls performed during the aseptic filling of
vials and syringes. The operator will apply this procedure in conjunction with the filling
operation described in SOP-MFG-001. Analytical release testing is performed separately under
SOP-QC-001 and is not covered here.

## 3. Responsibilities

The filling operator will perform the in-process checks described here and the results are
recorded in the batch record. The line lead will confirm that checks are performed at the
required frequency. Out-of-limit results will be escalated to the area supervisor without
delay.

## 4. Materials and Equipment

The operator will use the calibrated in-process balance, the container closure integrity test
apparatus where applicable, and the approved in-process control worksheet. Only calibrated
instruments will be used, and calibration status is verified before each batch.

## 5. In-Process Checks

1. Fill weight is checked at the start of filling, at defined intervals during the run, and at
   the end of the batch; results are compared against the approved target and range.
2. The balance will be challenged with a reference weight before use and the check is
   recorded.
3. Container closure will be assessed so that stoppers or plungers are confirmed to be fully
   seated, and defective units are removed from the batch.
4. Product appearance is examined for evidence of particulate matter or fill defects at each
   check point.
5. All results are documented contemporaneously in the batch record.

## 6. Handling of Out-of-Limit Results

When a fill-weight result is found to be outside the approved range, filling will be paused and
the fill volume is adjusted before filling is resumed. Affected units are quarantined and a
deviation is initiated. The validation basis for the in-process limits is described in
SOP-VAL-001 and will be consulted when the significance of a result is assessed.
