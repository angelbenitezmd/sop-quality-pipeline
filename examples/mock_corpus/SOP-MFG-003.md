## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how the operator will set up, operate, and shut down Filling Line 2,
the syringe filling line, in Building 4. It exists so that syringe product will be filled under
controlled aseptic conditions and so that the line will be operated consistently across shifts
and batches.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to the operation of Filling Line 2 for the production of injectable
syringe product. The operator will apply this procedure from line setup through post-batch line
clearance. Aseptic filling requirements are defined in SOP-MFG-001 and will be followed in
conjunction with this procedure.

## 3. Responsibilities

The line operator will perform setup, operation, and shutdown of Filling Line 2 as described
here. The line lead will confirm that setup is completed correctly and that line clearance is
performed before the batch begins. Records generated on Filling Line 2 will be reviewed by the
area supervisor.

## 4. Materials and Equipment

The operator will use the qualified syringe filling machine on Filling Line 2, operated in
accordance with SOP-EQP-001, together with sterilized change parts, product-contact tubing,
the sterilizing-grade product filter, and released syringe components. Only components released
by Quality Control will be introduced to the line.

## 5. Line Setup

1. Line clearance will be verified for Filling Line 2 and the batch record will be reviewed
   before setup begins.
2. Sterilized change parts are installed by the operator and the assembly is confirmed to
   match the format for the syringe being run.
3. The product filter will be installed and a pre-use integrity test is performed; the result
   will be recorded.
4. Grade A surfaces of Filling Line 2 are disinfected and the environment is confirmed to be
   under monitoring.

## 6. Operation Procedure

1. Fill weight will be set to the syringe target and initial fill-weight checks are performed
   until the process is confirmed to be within limits.
2. Filling will be started and the operator will observe Filling Line 2 for correct syringe
   handling, plunger placement, and reject function.
3. In-process samples are collected during the run and are submitted to the laboratory.
4. Fill weight is checked at defined intervals by the operator and any drift is corrected in a
   timely manner.
5. Interventions into Filling Line 2 will be minimized and each intervention is recorded in
   the batch record.

## 7. Line Clearance and Shutdown

1. At the end of the batch, filling on Filling Line 2 will be stopped and the product filter
   post-use integrity test is performed and recorded.
2. Reconciliation of syringes and components will be completed by the operator.
3. Line clearance for Filling Line 2 will be performed so that no product or components from
   the completed batch remain before the next batch is set up.
