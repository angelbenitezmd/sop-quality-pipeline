PURPOSE AND APPLICABILITY

The purpose of this standard operating procedure is to describe, in a manner that is intended
to be sufficient for the generation of defensible cleaning validation data, the sampling and
swab recovery activities that shall be performed in support of the cleaning validation program
of Building 4, and it shall be understood that the activities described herein, which include
the collection of swab samples and rinse samples from the product contact surfaces of the
equipment that has been subjected to the cleaning cycles that are described within SOP-CLN-002,
shall be performed only by personnel who have been appropriately trained and who have been
determined to be competent in the technique of swab sampling in accordance with the applicable
training requirements, and it shall be further understood that the recovery factors that are
applied to the analytical results shall have been established during the recovery studies that
are described within SOP-CLN-099 prior to the use of any sampling location in a validation
exercise.

SCOPE

This procedure shall be applied to the cleaning validation sampling of all product contact
surfaces that are identified within the applicable cleaning validation protocol, and it shall
be applied to both the swab sampling of defined surface locations and the collection of final
rinse samples, and it shall be applied as necessary and as frequently as is deemed to be
appropriate in order that the state of control of the cleaning process is demonstrated.

RESPONSIBILITIES

It is the responsibility of the validation personnel that the sampling activities shall be
performed in accordance with this procedure and the applicable protocol, and it is the
responsibility of the Quality Control function that the analytical determination of the swab
and rinse samples shall be performed in accordance with SOP-QC-004, and it is the
responsibility of the Validation Lead that the sampling locations, the acceptance limits, and
the applicable recovery factors shall be reviewed and approved prior to the execution of any
sampling event.

MATERIALS AND EQUIPMENT

The following shall be utilized: pre-wetted low-particulate swabs of a type that has been
qualified for the surface that is being sampled, an appropriate quantity of a suitable
extraction solvent, sample containers that have been demonstrated to be free of interfering
substances, a sampling template that defines an area that is considered to be adequate for the
recovery of residues, and personal protective equipment, and it shall be ensured that the
materials that are used are consistent with those that were used during the establishment of
the recovery factors.

SAMPLING PROCEDURE

1. The sampling locations shall be identified in accordance with the applicable cleaning
   validation protocol and it shall be confirmed that the surfaces to be sampled are dry and
   that the cleaning cycle that is described within SOP-CLN-002 has been completed and
   documented.
2. A defined area, which is ordinarily 25 square centimeters unless a different area is deemed
   to be appropriate and is specified within the protocol, shall be delineated using the
   sampling template, and the swab shall be applied to the delineated area in a systematic and
   overlapping manner in one direction and subsequently in a direction that is perpendicular
   to the first, in order that the recovery of residues from the surface is maximized to the
   extent that is practicable.
3. The swab shall be transferred to the sample container without delay and the container shall
   be labeled with the sampling location, the date, and the initials of the sampler in
   accordance with SOP-DOC-001.
4. A final rinse sample shall be collected from the defined sampling point where rinse
   sampling is specified, and the sample shall be handled in a manner that is appropriate so
   that the integrity of the sample is preserved during transport to the laboratory.
5. The recovery factor that was established during the recovery studies that are described
   within SOP-CLN-099 shall be applied to the analytical result in order that the quantity of
   residue that is present on the surface is estimated in a manner that accounts for the
   incomplete recovery that is inherent in the swab sampling technique.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA AND RECOVERY

The recovery that is obtained for a given surface and residue combination shall be considered
to be acceptable when the mean recovery is not less than the value that is defined within the
recovery study, and any sampling location for which the recovery is determined to be
insufficient shall be evaluated and a correction shall be applied or an alternative approach
shall be adopted as required, and the residue result that is calculated following the
application of the recovery factor shall be compared against the acceptance limit that has
been established in accordance with the applicable health-based exposure assessment.

RELATED DOCUMENTS AND REFERENCES

This procedure shall be read in conjunction with SOP-CLN-002, SOP-QC-004, SOP-CLN-099, and
SOP-DOC-001. The cleaning validation program is maintained in accordance with PDA TR-70 and
EU GMP Annex 15 (2001 revision).
