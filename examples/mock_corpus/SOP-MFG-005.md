## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how the operator will perform the washing, siliconization, and
sterilization of elastomeric stoppers so that stoppers of suitable quality will be provided to
the aseptic filling lines. It exists so that particulate and bioburden levels will be reduced
to appropriate levels and so that stoppers will be rendered sterile before use.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all elastomeric vial stoppers processed in Building 4. The operator
will apply this procedure as necessary whenever stoppers are prepared for a filling campaign.
Stoppers that are not processed in accordance with this procedure will not be released to the
filling suite.

## 3. Responsibilities

The stopper processing operator will perform the steps described here and adequate records
will be maintained in a timely manner. The area supervisor will confirm that the process was
completed correctly. Sterilization will be performed using the autoclave operated in
accordance with SOP-EQP-002, and any stoppers that are to be dried under vacuum will be
processed using the equipment described in SOP-EQP-005.

## 4. Materials and Equipment

The operator will use the automated stopper washer, water-for-injection, the silicone emulsion
prepared at the appropriate concentration, sterilization-grade bags or the qualified load
configuration, and the autoclave. Only stoppers released by Quality Control will be used, and
components will be handled in a manner that is suitable to prevent recontamination.

## 5. Procedure

1. Stoppers will be loaded into the washer and the wash cycle is initiated; the stoppers are
   washed with water-for-injection until the effluent is confirmed to be adequate.
2. Silicone emulsion is applied to the stoppers as required so that machinability on the
   filling line is achieved; excess silicone will be avoided.
3. The stoppers are transferred to the sterilization load and the load is configured in the
   qualified pattern.
4. Sterilization will be performed in the autoclave in accordance with SOP-EQP-002, and the
   cycle parameters are monitored throughout.
5. Following sterilization, the stoppers are dried and are cooled as necessary before transfer
   to the filling suite; the sterilized load will be protected from contamination during
   transfer.
6. Processing will be documented and the records are reviewed periodically by the area
   supervisor.

## 6. In-Process Controls

Bioburden and endotoxin levels will be monitored at a suitable frequency and results will
remain within the established limits. Load configuration will be verified before each
sterilization cycle. Hold times between processing and use will be observed as required, and
any excursion will be evaluated in a timely manner.
