I. Purpose

The purpose of this standard operating procedure is the establishment of a consistent
methodology for the performance of the quantitative determination of drug product potency
by means of reversed-phase high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC), such that the
generation of assay results, the interpretation of those results, and the disposition of
the associated batches are conducted under a state of analytical control that provides
assurance of the reliability, the accuracy, and the reproducibility of the potency values
that are reported for release and stability purposes.

II. Scope

The applicability of this procedure extends to the performance of potency assay of all
sterile injectable drug products that are manufactured at Building 4, including both vial
and pre-filled syringe presentations, and it governs the conduct of assay for release
testing, for stability testing, and, where appropriate, for the investigation of
out-of-specification occurrences. The exclusion of identification testing, of related-
substance profiling, and of content-uniformity determination from the scope of this
procedure must be observed, given that the execution of those determinations is addressed
under separate analytical procedures as necessary.

III. Responsibilities

The QC analyst must undertake the performance of the assay, the preparation of the
standard and sample solutions, and the documentation of all observations in accordance
with SOP-DOC-001. The QC reviewer must conduct an assessment of the completeness and the
correctness of the data package prior to the approval of the reported result. The QC
Laboratory Manager must ensure the provision of adequate training and the maintenance of
the qualified status of the instrumentation, and, in a timely manner, must perform a review
of any deviation that arises during the execution of this procedure.

IV. Materials, Reagents, and Instrumentation

The following must be available for the conduct of the assay: an HPLC system equipped with
a quaternary or binary pump, a temperature-controlled autosampler, a column compartment
capable of the maintenance of a suitable temperature, and an ultraviolet or photodiode-
array detector; a C18 analytical column of appropriate dimensions and particle size; HPLC-
grade acetonitrile and water; a mobile phase prepared through the combination of an aqueous
buffer and organic modifier in the ratio that is specified in the applicable analytical
control document; and a reference standard of established potency the traceability of which
must be documented. The verification of the calibration of the analytical balance that is
used for the weighing of the reference standard must be confirmed by reference to
SOP-QC-006 before any weighing is undertaken.

V. Performance of System Suitability Verification

Prior to the injection of any sample solution, the performance of a verification of system
suitability must be undertaken through the injection of a series of replicate injections of
the standard solution, and the acceptability of the chromatographic system must be
established through the demonstration that the relative standard deviation of the peak-area
response of not fewer than five replicate injections of the standard solution does not
exceed 2.0 percent, that the tailing factor of the principal peak is within a range that is
considered suitable, and that the number of theoretical plates is adequate, whereby, in the
event that any one of these parameters is found to be outside of the established acceptance
criteria, the continuation of the analytical run must not be permitted and an assessment of
the assignable cause must be conducted in a timely manner before any further injection is
performed. The validation of this analytical procedure has been executed in accordance with
ICH Q2(R1), and the ongoing demonstration of the maintenance of the validated state must be
provided through the periodic performance of system-suitability verification as required.

VI. Procedure

1. The preparation of the standard solution must be performed through the accurate weighing
   of a suitable quantity of the reference standard and the subsequent dissolution and
   dilution of that quantity in the diluent to the concentration that is prescribed in the
   analytical control document.
2. The preparation of the sample solution must be performed through the withdrawal of a
   representative aliquot of the drug product and the dilution of that aliquot to a nominal
   concentration that is comparable to that of the standard solution, and the performance of
   this preparation must be conducted in duplicate.
3. The equilibration of the chromatographic system must be achieved through the passage of
   mobile phase until a stable baseline is obtained.
4. Following the successful completion of the performance of system-suitability
   verification, the injection of the standard and sample solutions must proceed in the
   sequence that is defined in the sequence table, with the interspersion of bracketing
   standards at appropriate intervals so that the demonstration of the stability of the
   system response throughout the run is provided.
5. Upon the completion of the analytical run, the review of the chromatograms and the
   integration of the peaks of interest must be performed, and any manual adjustment of the
   integration must be documented with a justification in accordance with SOP-DOC-001.

VII. Calculation and Acceptance Criteria

The calculation of the potency must be performed through the comparison of the peak-area
response of the sample solution against the peak-area response of the standard solution,
with the incorporation of the potency of the reference standard, the dilution factors, and
the label claim, and the result must be expressed as a percentage of the label claim. The
reporting of a result that lies within the range of 95.0 percent to 105.0 percent of the
label claim is considered to represent a conforming outcome unless a product-specific limit
that differs from this range has been established, in which event the product-specific limit
must take precedence. The release of any batch is contingent upon the demonstration of
conformance in accordance with 21 CFR 211.165.

VIII. Data Review and Documentation

The documentation of all raw data, of all calculations, and of all chromatographic records
must be maintained within the validated chromatography data system, and the performance of
a second-person review must be undertaken prior to the approval of the result. Any
occurrence of an atypical result must be subjected to an assessment in accordance with the
laboratory investigation process in a timely manner.
