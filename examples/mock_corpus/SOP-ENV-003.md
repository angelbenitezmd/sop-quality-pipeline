## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines how to investigate environmental monitoring (EM) excursions in
Building 4. It exists so that each excursion is assessed, contained, and closed with a
clear conclusion.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to every alert-limit and action-limit excursion found during
viable, non-viable, or personnel monitoring in classified areas.

## 3. Responsibilities

EM analysts must report each excursion at once. The EM Program Lead must own the
investigation. Production must protect any affected batch. Quality Assurance must
approve the risk assessment and the final disposition.

## 4. Immediate Response

1. Flag the excursion in the EM system on the day of readout.
2. Notify the area supervisor and Quality Assurance within one hour.
3. Secure the affected room, plates, and records.
4. Place any potentially affected batch on hold.

## 5. Investigation Steps

1. Open an investigation record for the excursion.
2. Identify the recovered organism to the required taxonomic level.
3. Review cleaning, personnel, and access records for the affected period.
4. Compare the result against recent trends for the same site.
5. Determine the most probable root cause.

## 6. Risk Assessment

1. Assess the impact on product quality using the site risk method.
2. Apply the quality risk management principles of ICH Q9(R1).
3. Rate the excursion by severity and by likelihood of recurrence.
4. Confirm the disposition with sterility results from SOP-QC-002.

## 7. Corrective Action and Closure

1. Define corrective actions that address the root cause.
2. Assign an owner and a due date to each action.
3. Verify that each action is complete and effective.
4. Record the investigation per SOP-DOC-001.
5. Update the monitoring plan under SOP-ENV-001 when the cause requires it.
