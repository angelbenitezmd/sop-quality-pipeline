## Purpose

- This procedure defines the handling of serialization and aggregation data for saleable units packaged at Building 4.
- The Packaging & Labeling function is responsible for ensuring that unique identifiers are applied, verified, and aggregated in compliance with track-and-trace requirements.

## Scope

- This procedure applies to serialized cartons and aggregated shippers for vial and syringe product.

## Responsibilities

- The Packaging Supervisor is responsible for ensuring that serial numbers are provisioned against an approved packaging order.
- The Serialization Operator is responsible for ensuring that each unit is marked with a legible, unique identifier and that the code is verified by the vision system.
- Serialization Support is responsible for ensuring that data is exchanged with the central serialization system and that access is controlled.
- Quality Assurance is responsible for ensuring that serialization records are reviewed before release.

## Procedure

- Serial numbers are requested from the central system for the required quantity.
- Each carton is marked with a 2D data matrix carrying the product code, serial number, lot, and expiry.
- The vision system verifies each code, and unreadable or duplicate codes are rejected and decommissioned.
- Cartons are aggregated to shippers, and the parent–child relationship is recorded.
- Commissioned, decommissioned, and reconciled serial numbers are reported to the central system at batch closure.

## Data Integrity

- Serialization data is stored in a validated system with an audit trail in accordance with SOP-DOC-002.
- Access is role-based, and each action is attributable to a unique user.
